How it works

From zero to managed forwarding in 3 steps

No servers to maintain. Everything runs on Cloudflare's free tier, inside your own account.

1

Deploy to Cloudflare

One click deploys the Worker, database and storage into your own account. Your data never leaves your control.

2

Connect your domain

Enable Email Routing, verify a destination address, and set catch-all to the InboxFly Worker — the panel guides you through each step.

3

Mail gets managed

Every incoming mail is forwarded, archived with full copy and attachments, searchable, and counted in your stats.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does my data live?

In your own Cloudflare account — D1 database, R2 storage and the Worker all belong to you. InboxFly's panel is just an interface; revoke its API access anytime and your data stays.

What does it cost?

The software is free (MIT). Everything runs on Cloudflare's free tier: Workers 100k requests/day, D1 5GB, R2 10GB storage, Email Routing free. Beyond that, Cloudflare's standard rates apply.

Will forwarded mail land in spam?

Cloudflare Email Routing forwards via their own infrastructure with SPF aligned. Deliverability is generally good, but we publish sending-side guidance in the docs just in case. Note: "Forwarded" status means Cloudflare accepted it — the final mailbox placement isn't observable by any forwarding service.

Do I need to trust InboxFly with my mail?

No. Once deployed, mail flows directly through Cloudflare's Email Routing into the Worker in your account. The panel only manages configuration and displays stored copies — revoke its API token and the mail pipeline keeps running.

Cloud vs self-hosted?

Self-hosted (this page) runs on your own account for free. InboxFly Cloud — a hosted version with extra features like AI analysis — is coming soon. This site will switch to the Cloud homepage when it launches.
