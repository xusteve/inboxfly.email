About InboxFly

What is InboxFly?

InboxFly is an open-source email forwarding management tool built for Cloudflare Email Routing. It adds the missing layer on top of Cloudflare’s free email forwarding: a real management panel with rules, full copies, attachments, search and per-rule statistics.

Why we built it

Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your mail for free, but gives you no management interface — no rule editor with filters, no searchable archive, no statistics. We built InboxFly to close that gap, with one firm principle: your mail stays in your own Cloudflare account.

Our principles

  • Open source — MIT licensed, the code can be audited and self-hosted freely.
  • Data ownership — D1, R2 and the Worker live in your account; InboxFly holds no mail content.
  • Privacy first — remote tracking pixels are blocked, no analytics on your mail, no mandatory third-party services.
  • Free to start — runs entirely within Cloudflare’s free tier.

Product family

  • InboxFly Self-Hosted — open-source edition, deploy to your own Cloudflare account.
  • InboxFly Cloud — hosted edition with AI analysis and SMTP relay (coming soon).
